# Gadget Store OS Store Guides

Use your own account. The screens available to you depend on your organization, role, assigned store or branch, and the permissions selected by the owner.

## Owner

1. Sign in and review the dashboard.
2. Open **Settings → Organization** to configure the store tree, branches, staff and manager permissions.
3. Create product definitions, then add suppliers and purchase orders.
4. Receive stock into the correct branch. Enter the IMEI or serial number for each serialized device; use quantities for accessories.
5. Start a stock count, select its location, scan or count each line, review differences and complete reconciliation.
6. Use **Point of Sale** to select products, customer and payment. Check the saved sale and receipt result.
7. Review customers, supplier activity, finance, reports, staff attendance and **Audit Log**.

## Manager

1. Sign in and check your assigned organization and store context.
2. Use the dashboard to review sales, stock and operational alerts.
3. Receive or move stock, scan device identifiers and run stock counts when your permissions allow.
4. Record sales and customer details at checkout. Use the returns workflow for returns.
5. Use purchasing, staff, attendance and reports only when those permissions are available.
6. Ask the owner to grant or remove permissions. Do not share accounts or work around an access denial.

## Staff

1. Sign in with your account and check the selected store or branch.
2. Find stock in **Inventory** or **Device Registry**. Scan a barcode or IMEI when available, or enter it manually.
3. Use **Point of Sale** to add items, select the customer and record payment accurately.
4. Clock in and out in **Attendance** when enabled for your role.
5. Report damaged, missing or unexpected stock to your manager. Do not adjust counts unless authorized.
6. Owner settings, staff permissions and other restricted areas are unavailable to staff without the required permission.

## Daily stock workflow

Receive incoming goods against the supplier and purchase record. Confirm the branch and quantity. For a phone or other serialized device, verify its identifier and model before saving. At sale, confirm the item, customer and payment. If a scan is unavailable, enter the identifier manually and verify it before saving. Report any mismatch so a manager can review the stock record and audit history.
